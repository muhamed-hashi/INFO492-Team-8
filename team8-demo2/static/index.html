What the reference check can establish

Sheet numbers are checked against 206 sheets listed or cited in the six Dett addenda. A match does not verify a detail or the latest revision, and the full original drawing set was not available, so a missing match should be confirmed by a person. The AI judges fit from sheet titles only.
