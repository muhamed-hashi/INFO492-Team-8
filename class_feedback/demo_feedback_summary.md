Across both demos, Team 8 received the most peer reviews in the class and stood out for intellectual honesty and clear role boundaries: the agent flags problems, the subcontractor decides, and the agent never speaks for the GC or architect. Demo 1 was praised for openly calling the 25% reduction a research target and acknowledging that a walkthrough isn't evidence of faster review, but that honesty left the room without a measurable result, and several reviewers felt the claim couldn't be judged. Demo 2 answered that critique with the strongest experimental design of the week, comparing code-only, AI-only, and code-plus-AI conditions across 24 drafts (12 with planted errors, 12 clean), measuring both misses and false alarms, and showing code-plus-AI caught all 12 problems. The main criticisms of Demo 2 were that presenters were too quiet to hear, the improvements since Demo 1 weren't framed as a clear before-and-after, and the core claim about reduced GC review time remains untested on real project data. For Demo 3, the feedback recommends opening with a single slide showing what changed and the new result, speaking louder or using a mic, and directly timing reviewers with and without the agent to test the review-time claim.
